![](/img/Autumnal-Equinox-2026-flyer.jpg)Celebrating the Autumnal Equinox, the Zazen Day will begin at 6:30am at Earth+Sky Zen Dublin Dojo, and will end at 6:30pm. It is a time to practice equilibrium and balance, in all the aspects of our lives including our sitting practice and walking meditation. Participants may join for some of the day if the early start is not possible. Just let us know in advance by email. The suggested donation is €35.
