![](/img/dojo.jpeg)We will open for Sunday evening zazen on September 6th at 6:15pm. There will be a introduction for newcomers at 5:30pm.

The times for evening zazen on Wednesdays and Sundays remains the same, at 6:15pm, morning zazen will now be a dawn sitting, and the times will change as the year progresses towards Winter Solstice. 

The in-person dawn sitting from Sept. 7th to Sept. 11th will be from 6am - 6:40am followed by the morning ceremony, which will also be offered online: Meeting ID 835 5345 4120 Passcode watermoon

No morning zazen on Wednesday, Sept 9th that week.
