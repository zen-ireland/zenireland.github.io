Our first sesshin of the New Year will occur on the St. Brigid's Day weekend, the ancient festival of Early Spring - Imbolg -. There will be a Sewing Workshop on Saturday, January 30th, 2027, and a Zazen Day on Sunday, January 31st. Saturday's workshop is an opportunity for those of you sewing a rakasu or kesa to join with others in the traditional practice of sewing zen robes. It's also an opportunity to learn to sew if you don't know how, or to practice a traditional contemplative craft. Sunday's Zazen Day will be an opportunity to practice in a silent and calm atmosphere, in contemplation of the Divine Feminine, deep within all of us whatever our gender, and in memory of our Matron Saint, Brigid.
