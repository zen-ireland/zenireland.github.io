![](/img/Autumnal-Equinox-2026-flyer.jpg "Autumnal Equinox 2026")The Zazen day is an opportunity for the practice of equilibrium and balance, both physical and mental. It will be a day of sitting meditation, walking meditation, and some samu/meitheal around the preparation and serving of breakfast and lunch. There will be a dharma talk and discussion. All are welcome. Participants may come for part of the day, if the full day is not feasible for you. The suggested donation is €35, but no one should stay for lack of funds. Please register by email before Sept. 15th.
